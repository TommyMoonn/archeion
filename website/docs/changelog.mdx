## v1.8.0 {#release-1-8-0}

November 14, 2026

- Pin Book details beside the Library to keep a selected book's information and actions in view. With no book selected, the panel offers your last-read book when it can be resumed. Narrow windows use a slide-over panel.
- Resize the Library sidebar and Book details by dragging their edges or using the keyboard. Your widths and pin preference are saved.
- Archeion has a new logo and desktop icons, plus refreshed Dark and Light colors for the application and Reader. Custom themes and the Sepia Reader theme remain available.
- Five optional themes are available to [download and import](https://github.com/TommyMoonn/archeion/tree/main/themes): Archeion Nocturne, Mono, Chalk, Classic Dark, and Classic Light. The Classic themes restore the previous built-in colors. Import them through Settings → Appearance → Manage themes; choose their Reader colors separately under Reader theme.
- The Library keeps its layout through loading, searching, and empty results, and returning from Reader restores your scroll position and keyboard focus. Update notifications stay within their own area.
- Dialogs keep their size and button labels steady while work is in progress. Loading content stays inside the dialog, with retry options when it fails, and keyboard focus carries through to the loaded controls.
- Cover replacement now uses the Write cover to EPUB button without a separate confirmation checkbox. You can preview the image before writing it, with progress and recovery feedback during the operation.
- Dialogs, side panels, and the Library sidebar have consistent opening and closing transitions that respect reduced-motion preferences. Selection feedback, font pickers, and Settings layouts have also been refined.
- Application and utility windows apply your saved appearance before showing their content. New utility windows open on the main window's display, and windows stranded off-screen are brought back into reach.
- Book opening, returning to the Library, and archive switching keep the surrounding interface visible while loading or saving finishes. Continuous Reader position capture now follows visible text across trailing chapter whitespace. Theme selection restores keyboard focus, and Reader progress previews stay inside the progress track.

[Full release notes for v1.8.0 ↗](https://github.com/TommyMoonn/archeion/releases/tag/v1.8.0)

## v1.7.0 {#release-1-7-0}

October 18, 2026

- Continuous reading now scrolls through chapter boundaries in both directions, including books with a short opening section or a large cover.
- Loading an earlier chapter or an image above your position no longer shifts the passage you are reading. Changing text size or resizing the window also keeps your place.
- Archeion's default book layout uses unindented paragraphs with space between them, balanced text margins, and centered standalone illustrations that fit the reading area.
- Choose Publisher under Book layout in Reader appearance controls to use a reflowable book's own paragraph spacing and layout. The choice is saved for that book, and your Reader font and theme still apply. Fixed-layout books keep their designed pages.
- Reader side panels slide over the book without changing its size. Panels open immediately while content loads, and switching panels keeps keyboard focus predictable. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Chapters far from your position are unloaded during Continuous reading and restored when you return, along with their annotations.

[Full release notes for v1.7.0 ↗](https://github.com/TommyMoonn/archeion/releases/tag/v1.7.0)

## v1.6.0 {#release-1-6-0}

October 9, 2026

- Install **1.6.0 manually once** to enable in-app updates for future releases. Existing 1.5.x versions cannot install this update from inside Archeion.
- Archeion automatically checks for stable updates. Turn this off in **Settings → General → Automatically check for updates** if you prefer manual checks.
- Available updates appear at the bottom-right of the Library without interrupting Reader. Choose **Later** to postpone that version's notification for 24 hours.
- **Update now** downloads and verifies the update while you keep using Archeion. Choose **Restart now** when it is ready to install and reopen the app; restarting is always your decision.
- Check for updates manually in **About**, even when automatic checks are off or a notification is postponed. Open release history with **Changelog** in About or **What's new** on an update notice.
- Choose installed Windows fonts for **Interface font** and **Display font** in Appearance settings using a searchable font picker.
- Reader's **Typeface** picker now includes installed Windows fonts independently of application fonts. Missing saved fonts use a fallback without erasing your choice; restart Archeion after installing or removing fonts to refresh the list.

[Full release notes for v1.6.0 ↗](https://github.com/TommyMoonn/archeion/releases/tag/v1.6.0)

## v1.5.6 {#release-1-5-6}

October 6, 2026

- Compare themes using compact previews beneath each theme name and a larger preview showing how the selected theme looks across navigation and workspace areas.
- Library and Archive Manager now have a flush navigation/workspace layout instead of a framed, inset workspace.
- The Library titlebar now contains the Archeion name and sidebar toggle. Open Quick Actions with **Ctrl + Shift + P**, or your configured shortcut.
- Use **Reveal archive folder** in the archive switcher to open the active archive's location.
- Custom themes retain clear navigation/workspace boundaries even when those areas use the same color. Theme warnings also check the readability of muted navigation text.
- Controls and labels use lighter text emphasis, and ordinary action icons have more consistent outlines. Text sizes, sidebar dimensions, Library headings, and Reader publication fonts are unchanged.
