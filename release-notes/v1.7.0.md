## Changes

- Continuous reading now scrolls through chapter boundaries in both directions, including books with a short opening section or a large cover.
- Loading an earlier chapter or an image above your position no longer shifts the passage you are reading. Changing text size or resizing the window also keeps your place.
- Archeion's default book layout uses unindented paragraphs with space between them, balanced text margins, and centered standalone illustrations that fit the reading area.
- Choose Publisher under Book layout in Reader appearance controls to use a reflowable book's own paragraph spacing and layout. The choice is saved for that book, and your Reader font and theme still apply. Fixed-layout books keep their designed pages.
- Reader side panels slide over the book without changing its size. Panels open immediately while content loads, and switching panels keeps keyboard focus predictable. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Chapters far from your position are unloaded during Continuous reading and restored when you return, along with their annotations.
