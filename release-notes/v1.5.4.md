## Changes

- Switching archives quickly now keeps your latest choice instead of showing an older result.
- Rescanning one archive no longer stops a scan of another archive.
- Storage maintenance in Settings no longer interrupts an active Library scan.
- Archive changes made in another window appear again after a temporary startup connection failure, without restarting Archeion.
